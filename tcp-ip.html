What is TCP/IP?

TCP/IP (Transmission Control Protocol/Internet Protocol) is the fundamental suite of communication protocols used to connect devices on the internet. It defines how data is packaged, addressed, transmitted, routed, and received.

Key Fact: TCP/IP is often called the "language of the internet". Without it, devices couldn't communicate.

TCP/IP handles data transmission across networks.

Two Main Protocols

  • TCP: Ensures reliable delivery by breaking data into packets and reassembling them at the destination.
  • IP: Handles addressing and routing so packets reach the correct destination.

Why It Matters

TCP/IP enables global communication, email, web browsing, file transfers, and more. Every time you go online, TCP/IP is at work.
